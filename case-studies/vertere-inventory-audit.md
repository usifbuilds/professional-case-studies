# Vértere Inventory Data Validation

## Context

In HBKU facilities and laboratory systems work, I audited chemical inventory records in Vértere LIMS by checking digital entries against physical laboratory stock and correcting confirmed discrepancies.

## Process

1. Review the assigned digital inventory records.
2. Verify identifiers and quantities against physical stock.
3. Record confirmed discrepancies.
4. Correct or escalate records through the approved process.
5. Recheck updates for consistency.

## Skills demonstrated

- Data validation
- Inventory auditing
- Attention to detail
- Working with a laboratory information management system
- Following controlled operational procedures

## Confidentiality boundary

No chemical inventory, room information, user data, screenshots, exports, or internal procedures are published here.
